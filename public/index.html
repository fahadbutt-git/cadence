        Keyboard shortcuts
        
                  
          
                  Option+Space
                  Read from cursor or pause
                  
          
                  Escape

                  Accept the suggestion for this paragraph

                  Undo, including accepted suggestions
                  
          
                  Cmd+Shift+H
                  Toggle heading
